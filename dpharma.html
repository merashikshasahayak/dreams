Program Overview

Diploma in Pharmacy

D.Pharma is a 2-year diploma program that provides foundational knowledge and practical skills in pharmaceutical sciences. This program is ideal for students who want to start their career in the pharmaceutical industry or healthcare sector quickly. Graduates can work as pharmacists, pharmacy technicians, or pursue further education in B.Pharma.

Enroll Now View Curriculum

Why Choose D.Pharma at Dreams?

The Diploma in Pharmacy program at Dreams Group of Colleges is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in pharmaceutical sciences and practical skills required for the pharmacy profession. The program is approved by the Pharmacy Council of India and affiliated with the Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh.

Our curriculum covers essential topics including pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacology, pharmaceutics, and hospital pharmacy. Students gain hands-on experience through well-equipped laboratories, practical training, and industry exposure. The program prepares graduates for immediate employment in retail pharmacies, hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, and healthcare settings.

  • Comprehensive foundation in pharmaceutical sciences
  • Practical training in well-equipped laboratories
  • Industry exposure and internship opportunities
  • Eligible for registration as a pharmacist
  • Pathway to B.Pharma program

Curriculum

Program Structure

Year 1: Foundations

Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmaceutics, Human Anatomy & Physiology, Pharmacognosy, Biochemistry, Pharmaceutical Mathematics.

Year 2: Advanced Studies

Pharmacology, Pharmaceutical Analysis, Community Pharmacy, Hospital Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy, Industrial Pharmacy, Drug Store Management.
