Program Overview

Bachelor of Commerce

B.Com is a 3-year undergraduate program that provides a comprehensive understanding of commerce, accounting, finance, and business management. This program is designed to equip students with the knowledge and skills required for careers in accounting, banking, financial services, and corporate management.

Enroll Now View Curriculum

Why Choose B.Com at Dreams?

The Bachelor of Commerce program at Dreams Group of Colleges is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in accounting, finance, taxation, and business management. Our curriculum is regularly updated to meet the changing needs of the commerce and business sectors, ensuring that graduates are well-prepared for the professional world.

Students gain practical knowledge through case studies, industry projects, and internships. Our experienced faculty members provide personalized guidance, helping students develop analytical skills, financial expertise, and business acumen. The program also prepares students for professional certifications such as CA, CMA, and CS.

  • Comprehensive coverage of accounting, finance, and taxation
  • Practical exposure through internships and projects
  • Preparation for professional exams (CA, CMA, CS)
  • Development of analytical and business skills
  • Placement assistance in corporate and financial sectors

Curriculum

Program Structure

Year 1: Foundations

Financial Accounting, Business Organization, Business Mathematics, Business Economics, Business Communication, Principles of Management.

Year 2: Core & Advanced

Corporate Accounting, Cost Accounting, Business Law, Income Tax, Marketing Management, Financial Management, Human Resource Management.

Year 3: Specialization & Project

Auditing, Advanced Accounting, Taxation, Entrepreneurship Development, Major Project, Internship, Environmental Studies.
